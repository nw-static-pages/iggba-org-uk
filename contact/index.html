Contact

The fastest way to reach the site is email: editor@iggba.org.uk.

Please use it for:

  • Corrections. Quote the article and the sentence you think is wrong, and if you can, the source that contradicts it.
  • Suggestions. A term that should be in the glossary, or a rule that deserves a briefing.
  • Press and research enquiries about how the site is written and sourced.

Please do not use it for:

  • Questions about a specific gambling account, withdrawal or dispute. This site is not an operator and cannot see your account. Contact the operator first; if that fails, the operator's terms name an alternative dispute resolution (ADR) provider.
  • Requests to list, review or link to a gambling website. The site does not carry commercial links.

If gambling is causing you problems, please do not wait for an email reply — the responsible gambling page lists free help that is available right now, 24 hours a day.
