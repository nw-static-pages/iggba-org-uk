Privacy policy

This site collects as little as it can. There are no accounts, no newsletter and no advertising network.

What is collected

  • Server logs. The web server records the IP address, time, requested page and browser type of each visit for security and error diagnosis. Logs are rotated and deleted automatically after a short period and are not used to build profiles.
  • Email. If you write to us, your email address and message are kept for as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of corrections.

What is not collected

No cookies are set by this site itself and no analytics script runs on it. No data is sold or shared with advertisers. No gambling operator receives any information about visitors.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Requests go to the address on the contact page.

Links to other sites

Articles link to the Gambling Commission, GAMSTOP, GamCare and GambleAware. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
